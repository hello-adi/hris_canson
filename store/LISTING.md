## Detailed description

```
Ever open a shopping tab and wake up mid-checkout?

Hris Canson is a tiny browser extension that interrupts you on the sites you choose — with a dry, “have a seat” moment long enough to rethink the click.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Add a website (amazon.com, your usual rabbit holes, etc.)
2. Visit it
3. Chris intervenes
4. Write a short confession, then continue if you still want to

FEATURES
• Watchlist of sites you control
• Categories (Shopping, Social, Entertainment, Other) with matching intervention tone
• Random one-liners so it doesn’t feel like the same lecture every time
• Confession gate — type it out before you proceed
• Runs entirely in your browser

PRIVACY
No analytics. No tracking. No accounts. No servers of ours.
Your list stays in browser storage. Confessions are not saved.

WHAT IT IS NOT
Not a full site blocker. Not parental controls. Not a productivity suite with eighteen settings.
It’s one joke with one job: interrupt impulse long enough for you to choose on purpose.

Take a seat and rethink your choices.
```

## Single purpose (Chrome form)
